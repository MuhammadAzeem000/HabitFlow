## Full description (max 4,000)

```
HabitFlow is a fast, simple habit tracker that helps you stay consistent without the guilt.

Open the app, see today's habits, tap to check in, and get on with your day. HabitFlow works fully offline, needs no account, and never makes you wait for a sign-in, a network connection or an ad before you can check in.

ONE TAP TO CHECK IN
• Today's habits are ready the moment the app opens
• Tap once to mark a habit done, with instant feedback
• Changed your mind? Undo in one tap
• Accidental double taps are ignored

FLEXIBLE SCHEDULES
• Every day, specific days of the week, or a weekly goal (e.g. 3 times a week)
• Choose an icon and colour for each habit
• Drag and drop to put your habits in the order you like

PROGRESS WITHOUT PRESSURE
• Streaks that only count the days a habit is scheduled
• Weekly consistency, a kinder measure than streaks alone
• Take a rest day or pause a habit, and your streak isn't broken
• Supportive messages, never shame

SEE YOUR HISTORY
• Monthly calendar of every check-in
• Filter by habit, tap any day for details
• Current streak, best streak and this week's consistency for each habit

GENTLE REMINDERS
• Optional reminders at the times you choose
• Today's reminder is skipped once you've already checked in
• Follows your phone's 12- or 24-hour clock

FREE 30-DAY CHALLENGE
• Pick a habit and complete it on 30 days
• Missed days never reset your progress
• Pause or restart anytime, and it's free from start to finish

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Everything is stored on your device
• No account, no sign-in, no tracking
• Works in airplane mode

Light and dark themes, large-text friendly, and screen-reader labels throughout.
```
