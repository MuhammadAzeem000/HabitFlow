HabitFlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

HabitFlow ("the app") is a habit tracker built so that your data stays on your device. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

Summary

  • Your habits, check-ins, history and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The app does not collect, send, sell or share personal data.
  • The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising in this version.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, in a private database that only the app can read:

  • Habits you create (name, optional note, icon, colour, schedule, start date, reminder time, pause dates)
  • Your check-ins, rest days and challenge progress
  • Your settings (theme, time format, reminders on/off)

This information never leaves your device through the app. It is removed when you delete habits in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Android's own device backup may include app data if you have enabled it in your phone's settings; that backup is managed by Google under your Google account, not by HabitFlow.

Information we collect

None. The app does not connect to any server operated by us, and we do not receive any information about you or how you use the app.

Notifications

If you set a reminder, the app schedules a notification locally on your device using Android's alarm and notification system. The app asks for notification permission only when you first set a reminder. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings at any time.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show reminders you set.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to keep your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): used by notifications.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Advertising and future changes

This version contains no advertising. A future version may show non-intrusive ads in the free version, with an optional paid upgrade that removes them. Before that happens, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what the advertising provider collects, and the Google Play listing will be updated accordingly.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
